HOW THE RAIN OUTLOOK WORKS

Pressure, forecasts,
and local history.

The rain outlook combines three sources: pressure measured on your device, how often it has rained here before, and a weather forecast.

25%

Your pressure

Altitude-corrected pressure trends, with available climate and moisture context.

10%

Local history

Historical rain frequency for the area, season, and time of day.

65%

Model forecasts

Apple Weather precipitation forecasts, with Open-Meteo as a fallback when Apple Weather is unavailable.

Default weights when all three inputs are available. The blend changes when data is missing. Percentages describe the blend, not forecast accuracy.

DATA AND PRIVACY

Where your
data lives.

From records that stay on your device to what syncs through iCloud. Here’s how Pocket Weather Station handles your data.

◉

Records stay on your device

Detailed observations and symptom records are stored on your device. Export observation and diagnostic records as a ZIP whenever you want to look at them.

⌁

Sharing between devices uses iCloud

Sharing between your own devices is on by default. It stores a device name, an approximate location, and five-minute pressure summaries in your private iCloud storage. Deleting data in the app does not remove these iCloud records; delete them from iCloud storage settings.

⊕

Permissions match features

Motion and location access are used to record and correct pressure. Health access is optional. To fetch weather and elevation, the app sends an approximate location to those services.

A few things to know.

Which devices can I use?+

The app targets iPhone and iPad on iOS/iPadOS 17 or later, with an optional Apple Watch companion on watchOS 10 or later. Local pressure measurement requires a supported built-in barometer. The Watch complements the phone; it is not a standalone forecast engine.

Does my phone measure temperature and humidity?+

Pressure comes from the supported device’s barometer. Temperature and relative humidity come from weather providers for your area; they are not local phone-sensor measurements.

What works without a connection?+

Local sensor recording, stored observations, and the bundled climate classification can remain useful without a live forecast connection once the needed location is available. Fresh forecasts, airport reports, historical downloads, and iCloud syncing require network access.

Can I turn an iPad into a desk station?+

Yes, on a device with a supported barometer. Keep it at a fixed location, enable Reference station mode, start recording, and open Station display. The full-screen view keeps the display awake while open. External power is recommended for long sessions.

Are background updates and alerts continuous?+

iOS controls background execution, widget refreshes, and Live Activities. Permissions, power settings, available data, and region affect updates and alerts. Continuous sensor collection and exact delivery times are not guaranteed.
