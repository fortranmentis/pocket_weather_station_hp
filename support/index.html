Support

Need help with Pocket Weather Station? Start with the answers below. If you can’t find what you need, get in touch.

Contact

Report a problem or ask a question by opening an issue on GitHub. Issues are public, so please don’t include personal information such as your exact location.

Open a GitHub issue

Requirements

iPhone or iPad with iOS/iPadOS 17 or later. Measuring pressure requires a device with a built-in barometer. The Apple Watch app requires watchOS 10 or later and a paired iPhone.

No pressure reading

Make sure Motion & Fitness access is allowed in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Motion & Fitness, and that the app has permission. Devices without a barometer can still show forecasts but can’t measure pressure.

Altitude looks wrong

Barometric altitude drifts as the weather changes. Calibrate it with a known altitude or the current sea-level pressure on the Outdoor screen.

The rain outlook is missing or limited

Forecasts need an internet connection and location access. Minute-by-minute rain alerts are available only where Apple Weather supports them. When some data is missing, the outlook uses what is available.

Recording, widgets, or alerts stop updating

iOS controls background activity, widget refreshes, and Live Activities. Low Power Mode, permissions, and battery settings affect them. For a desk station, keep Station display open and connect power.

My other devices don’t appear

Sign in to the same Apple Account with iCloud on each device, and turn on Use with other devices in the app’s Settings. Devices outside the selected range are shown without being blended into your trend.

Deleting your data

Choose Settings → Delete all data in the app. Records already shared through iCloud are deleted separately from iPhone Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage. See the Privacy Policy for details.
