Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Pocket Weather Station has no accounts, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking tools. The developer does not run a server for the app and does not receive your records. This policy explains where your data is stored and what is sent to other services so the app can work.

Stored on your device

Pressure, altitude, observations, calibrations, predictions, and symptom records are stored on your device.

Current automatic retention: raw pressure samples for about 48 hours, minute observations, calibrations, and predictions for 365 days, event logs for 90 days, and raw Apple Watch readings for 30 days.

To delete these records, choose Settings → Delete all data in the app, or delete the app.

Sharing between your devices (iCloud)

Use with other devices is on by default. When it is on, the app stores a device name (“iPhone” or “iPad” unless you change it), a location rounded to about 100 m, and five-minute pressure summaries in your private iCloud database. Only devices signed in to the same Apple Account can read it. The developer cannot access it.

Turning the setting off stops new uploads. Delete all data in the app does not delete records already in iCloud. The app removes its own older pressure summaries on a best-effort basis, but the device record has no automatic expiry.

To remove the app’s iCloud records, go to iPhone Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage, select Pocket Weather Station if it is listed, and delete its data. Apple’s iCloud terms and privacy policy apply to data stored in iCloud.

Weather, elevation, and station services

To fetch forecasts, history, elevation, and nearby station reports, the app sends location information to these services: Apple WeatherKit, Open-Meteo (forecast, historical weather, and elevation), and the NOAA Aviation Weather Center (airport reports).

Forecast and history requests use coordinates rounded to two decimal places (about 1 km). Airport report requests send a surrounding area. Elevation requests use points on a grid of about 100 m around you.

Like any internet request, these services also receive your IP address. Their own privacy policies apply. Symptom records and raw sensor histories are never sent to them.

Health

Health access is optional. If you allow it, the app can read and save headache records. Other symptoms stay in the app.

Health data is not used for advertising, is not shared with third parties, and is not sent to weather services.

Permissions

Motion & Fitness: reads the barometer and detects movement, so pressure changes from walking or elevators can be separated from weather.

Location: finds local forecasts, nearby stations, and altitude references, and corrects pressure readings. If you choose Always, it also keeps pressure recording running in the background.

Notifications: pressure, rain, and departure alerts you turn on.

Widgets, Apple Watch, and Live Activities

Widgets, the Apple Watch app, and Live Activities use data shared on your devices. Symptom entries made on Apple Watch are sent to your iPhone. Live Activities do not use a push server.

Exports

ZIP exports you create can include observation locations, timestamps, and event details. You choose where to save or share them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. For questions, see the Support page.
